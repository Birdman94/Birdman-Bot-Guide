What this bot does

A Discord bot for Albion Online guilds: Calls to Arms, loot splits, regears, registration and payouts, built around your own Google Sheet.

The pages after this one: how a member uses it, how a night runs, officer setup, then every command. Commands only work inside a server where I'm set up — to use me in yours, message Birdman on Discord (thebirdman_94).

What it does by itself

Registration — A member runs /register and gets their guild or alliance role and nickname straight away; no officer is needed. Much of the bot relies on it. If an officer switches it on, a nightly check also finds who left the guild, tidies nicknames and tags, registers members whose Discord name is their in-game name, and asks the ones who haven't registered to /register in one message. Nobody gets a DM, and the nightly check never removes a role.
Signup threads — Once an officer starts a Call to Arms, its thread runs itself: members claim a numbered role from a drop-down menu (or by typing its number), and the bot keeps the standby list, start-time reminders, fill and bomb lists, no-show tracking and the recap.
Finding the deaths — For a regear request the bot finds the death on the killboard and keeps checking until it appears. After a loot split it checks for loot that never reached the chest.
Reports and posts — A weekly officer summary, weekly fame tracking, battleboard summaries, the objectives board, Siphoned Energy reminders, a welcome message for newcomers and nightly backups. The reports and posts are switched on in the settings menu and go where the officers choose.
Watching its own logs — Every day it checks that each log and post goes to a channel the right people can see, and tells the officers once when one doesn't. It never hides or moves a channel.
Members' own pages — /me shows a member their attendance, streak, sign-ups, wallet and Siphoned Energy balance. /alt links their other characters.

What waits for an officer's yes

Loot splits and pay — An officer logs a Call to Arms and the bot takes the guild's tax and splits the rest between everyone who came. Nobody is paid until an officer says so, and the bot never sends silver: it records what an officer sent. Every player has a wallet and everything is on record.
Regear — The bot prices a lost item, or a whole death from the killboard, at the cheapest market price for its quality in any city but Caerleon (or its tooltip EST if lower), and caps it by role. Members can file their own with a button; an officer approves it and marks it paid.
Taking roles away — A hard audit shows what it would change and asks before it removes a role. Officers can also register anyone who is stuck, and fix a record when Albion's lookup gets it wrong.
Changing a channel — If the Officer role can't see a log, the officers' note has a button that lets it in, after asking first. Nothing else about a channel is ever changed.
Settings — Only officers change settings, in one menu, and the things that act on their own are switched on there.
Extras — Click-an-emoji role panels, Siphoned Energy uploads, offtimers (side content), giveaways, recruiter rewards and an Excel report: officers start them.

Three ways to use it in a server

Full bot — Everything above, with that server's own separate data, settings and officers.
Register-only — Just registration: members verify their character and officers look after the roles.
CTA signups only — Just the numbered signup threads, built from the guild's own Google Sheet.

See it in action

What it is like to run a guild with the bot

Every screen on this page is the bot's own. The bot was run with made-up players and numbers and each message, menu and picture was captured exactly as it sent it. The few things that couldn't be captured (your own screenshots, the typed command) are drawn by hand and marked drawn. Albion's item pictures can't be shown here, so some pictures have no icons.

You set it up once. After that the bot posts the signups, reads your screenshots, finds deaths on the killboard, prices the gear, works out every share and keeps the lists. It asks before anything that moves silver or changes a role. It never sends silver in the game. It keeps each member's balance, and an officer pays it out whenever it suits both sides.

It reads your screenshots

Post the guild bank tab, the silver bags, the repair window and the party list. The bot reads the totals and the names, so nobody types a loot total or ticks forty names. A member the game can't confirm yet can register with a screenshot of their profile page. The reading runs on the bot's own computer; nothing is sent anywhere. It is an optional one-time install.

It does the lookups

Deaths come from the killboard, prices from the market, fights from Albion's battle list, and who is really in your guild from Albion itself. A member types their character name once, at /register.

It asks before it acts

Loot splits, payouts, regears and role removals show a preview first. Nothing is saved, paid or removed until an officer presses the button. If you want, the bot approves clear-cut regears by itself and says so on the ticket.

A CTA night, start to finish

Eight steps. The officer is needed for three of them.

  1. Post the signup/cta start with a tab of your sheet and a start time.🔒 Officer
  2. Members sign upA drop-down on the post. The roster and your sheet update as they click.🟢 Any member
  3. Reminders go outEveryone who signed up is pinged before the start. The server event is already on the Events list.🤖 The bot does this
  4. The fights are postedThe battleboard finds each fight, or the whole timer in one post.🤖 The bot does this
  5. Log the CTAPost the screenshots, press Read screenshots, check the preview, press Log it.🔒 Officer
  6. The split is worked outTax, cuts and every share, shown on a card in your loot channel. Each share goes onto the member's balance.🤖 The bot does this
  7. Pay out when it suits/pay pays out a member's whole balance in one go, or Mark shares paid on the card pays out the split. The bot only records it.🔒 Officer
  8. Everything closes upMembers are told what they are owed. The signup closes itself once every share is paid.🤖 The bot does this

CTA signups & builds

Signing up for a CTA

A CTA signup is one post. The roles come from a tab of your Google Sheet, one numbered row per role. Members claim a role from a drop-down, the bot keeps the roster up to date, and every pick is written back to the sheet.

1Start it with one command

🎟️ Starter role or officer

Choose the composition tab of your sheet, give it a title and a start time (UTC, Albion's clock). The time also creates the server event and sets the reminders.

2Members pick a role

🟢 Any member

The post shows the roster as a table: every role, who has it and what is still open. Members choose a role from the drop-down. Standby, Bomb and Fill are buttons, Builds shows the gear for each role, and Leave or Withdraw takes them off.

3The thread and the reminder

🤖 The bot does this

Each section of the composition gets its own card in the signup's thread, with who has claimed what. Before the start the bot reminds everyone who signed up and names the roles still open.

4After the CTA

🤖 The bot does this

When the loot is logged the bot posts a recap: who signed up and showed, who didn't, and who came without signing up.

The roster table is a picture the bot draws, so the colours always match the roles.

CTA lootsplit & loot check

Splitting the loot and checking the chest

After the fight an officer posts the screenshots and the bot does the sums. A loot check then shows who took loot and never put it in the chest.

1Post the screenshots

🔒 Officer

The guild bank tab, the repair window and the party list. A battle link works too, and then the attendance comes from the battle.

2Log a CTA and read the screenshots

🔒 Officer

Log a CTA in the officer menu asks for a name, the loot and the repair. 📷 Read screenshots then reads the pictures and shows what it found: the loot value, the repair cost and the people in the party list. Names it only half matched are listed so you can check them.

3Check the preview, then Log it

🔒 Officer

The preview is the whole split: tax, the guild's cut and each player's share (1,930,714 each for 14 people here). Nothing is saved until you press Log it.

4The loot card

🤖 The bot does this

The card in your loot channel lists every share and who is still to be paid. Mark shares paid pays them all out at once. Edit this split fixes a mistake, and everyone's share is recalculated.

5Who didn't deposit?

🔒 Officer

/lootcheck compares the Loot Logger export with the guild chest log. What someone died carrying is shown separately and isn't blamed on them. A loot thread can then chase the rest.

6Following up in the thread

🔒 Officer

A loot thread gives each player who still owes loot a card with Retry deathlog search and Resolved. The header counts what is open, and its buttons search again, ping the players or close the thread.

7Closing a thread

🔒 Officer

Close thread asks first. Closing stops the reminders, marks the header closed and archives the thread. Nothing is deleted or paid, the cards stay readable, and Reopen thread brings it back.

The loot check's "Worth knowing" notes mention the killboard because this example has no real killboard behind it.

Combined battleboard

Fight summaries without the links

The battleboard watches Albion's battle list for your guild. It posts each fight by itself, or one combined breakdown per CTA timer, so nobody pastes links.

1The fight is posted

🤖 The bot does this

A summary of the fight with the totals on both sides and the pages to flip through. Composition shows who brought what, Kill timeline the order of the kills, Momentum which side was winning and when.

2The tempo of the fight

🤖 The bot does this

Kill timeline shows how the fight went: who was killing and when. Momentum shows the swing. Together they show whether you lost the fight early or kept losing it for ten minutes.

3Who brought what

🤖 The bot does this

The composition page counts the weapons on each side. With a Builds tab in your sheet the bot also tells your own roles apart.

The fight is made up. The pictures are the ones the bot draws.

Officer menus & setup

What officers see

Officers work from one menu, /officer, and one list, /inbox. The first-time setup is a short checklist that the bot walks through with you.

1First-time setup

🔒 Officer

/help opens a checklist with a bar showing how far you are. Each page says what to do next and takes you to the right setting. You can leave and come back.

2The settings menu

🔒 Officer

Every setting is in one menu, grouped by what it does. The bot says where each one lives, so "turn this on in the settings menu" is never a puzzle.

3The officer menu

🔒 Officer

One menu for the jobs officers do most: log a CTA, look up a member, pay what is owed, upload the Siphoned Energy log. Anything that moves money asks first.

4Everything waiting on you

🔒 Officer

/inbox lists regears to approve, shares to pay and anything else that needs an officer, most pressing first. It only shows and links. Nothing happens until you press a button.

Automated regears

Regears without the spreadsheet

Regears are in silver. A member files a request, the bot finds the death and prices the gear, and an officer approves. The silver goes onto the member's balance, so nobody has to send anything first.

Silver regears only. The bot doesn't regear physical items. If your guild does, ask Birdman on Discord (thebirdman_94).

1A member files a request

🟢 Any member

File a regear opens a private thread. The member says what happened and can add a screenshot.

2The bot finds the death

🤖 The bot does this

It looks for the death on the killboard. If the killboard hasn't published it yet, the request waits: the bot looks again after a day, then daily for a week, and pings when it turns up. Check now looks straight away.

3The ticket

🤖 The bot does this

The ticket shows the death, the gear that was lost, whether the player was on a CTA, and what they have had in regears lately. Officers get the ping.

4Approve onto the balance

🔒 Officer

Approve & pay to wallet asks once, and says plainly that no silver is sent in the game. The amount goes onto the member's balance. If your officers would rather pay in the game straight away, Approve at guild rate and mark it paid afterwards. Both are in the same menu.

5The member sees it

🟢 Any member

/balance shows what is owed, including approved regears. It is paid out later with the rest, when it suits both sides.

The balance screens are in Member payouts.

2The report and one member

🔒 Officer

3Ping the members who are behind

🔒 Officer

4The reminder to upload again

🤖 The bot does this

When it has been a while, the bot asks the officers to upload again.

🤖 The bot does this

Member payouts

What members are owed and how it is paid

Members always know what they are owed, and officers pay it out whenever it suits. The bot never sends silver in the game. It keeps the balance, and an officer records each payment.

1A member checks

🟢 Any member

/balance shows what is owed, split into loot, regears and credits. /balances history lists where it came from. /me is the member's page.

2The bot tells them

🤖 The bot does this

Members owed silver get a weekly message, and the officers get a weekly summary.

3An officer pays out

🔒 Officer

/pay, or Pay what's owed in the member menu, pays out the member's whole balance: everything is marked paid and the balance goes to zero. The bot only records it; it never sends silver itself.

👤 For members

The few things most members ever do.

Get set up

  • /register — prove which Albion character is yours. You get your guild role and nickname.
  • /alt — link a second character. It can sign up too, and still counts as you. If your guild pays alt shares, each of your characters that was in the battle earns you its own loot share.
  • Registering matters: it's how the bot knows which Albion character is yours, so you're found when attendance is imported and your alts and page work. Stuck? An officer can register you. Until you do, the bot reminds you now and then (at most once a day) when you sign up or use a command.

Sign up for a CTA

  • On the signup post, pick your role from the Pick your role menu. Leave gives it back and Withdraw takes you off everything. Prefer typing? Type a role's number in the thread, e.g. 5 (-5 gives it back).
  • Every role taken? Press Standby (or type s): you get pinged when one opens, and you claim it from the menu or by typing its number. Press it again (or type -s) to leave.
  • Taking whatever's left? Press Fill: anything, or Fill: choose roles to pick the kinds you'd take (or type fill for anything), then make sure to claim a role for attendance and loot split; your caller may place you on an open one, and you're pinged at the start if one is still open. In a separate bomb squad that shares the loot split? Press Bomb (or type bomb). Press the button again (or type -bomb, -fill) to leave.
  • Signing up on an alt? After you pick a role, a private menu lets you choose which character goes on it. Or type 5 AltName.

Check your own numbers

  • Your guild may have a member home: a pinned message with a button each for your page, your balance, filing a regear, a price check, registering, adding an alt, the open CTAs and help. A button does exactly what the command does, and only you see the answer. Prefer typing? The commands below do the same.
  • /me — your attendance, streak, sign-ups, wallet and, if your guild tracks it, your Siphoned Energy balance (what you put in minus what you took out). Only you can see it. /me owed_dms:Off stops the weekly “you're owed silver” message.
  • /balances history — your wallet. /balance — what you're owed. /balances give — give some of your wallet to another player.
  • /cta info — how a CTA's loot was split. /balances leaderboard — the top players.
  • /route — type where you are and where you want to go; I show the shortest way between them on a map, and buttons swap the ends or avoid red or black zones. Only you see it.
  • /leaderboard — the top players for kills, K/D, (K+A)/D and most deaths over 7 days, 30 days or all time, counted from your guild's battles, with your own place under the table. Only you see it. Your guild has to have it switched on.
  • /price — what any item (gear, food, potions, resources...) costs in every city.
  • /mastery show — your level on each weapon and battlemount, from how often you have played it in the CTAs and offtimers. Open its tabs to see weapons or mounts, or search by name.
  • /builds show — the gear for your role: each piece with picture, tier and amount, and equal choices. Only you see it; Send me this in DMs keeps a copy.

If you died

  • Press File a regear on your guild's regear panel (or File a regear for this battle under a battle post). Pick your character, and the death if you have several. A thread opens for you and the officers with the killboard details. If the death isn't on the killboard yet it is checked again after 24 hours and then daily for a week, and you're pinged when it turns up. After a week, post a screenshot in the thread. You need to be registered first.

Choose what you get pinged for

  • Your guild may have a roles panel: a message with numbered emojis next to roles. Click an emoji to get that role (and its pings), click it again to drop it.

🎙️ Running a CTA

For officers, and anyone with the starter role (the callers). A typical night, in order.

Before it starts

  • /cta start — pick the composition tab. Add start_time:19:00 (UTC) for a reminder and a server event.
  • Add format:Roles only for just the number and the role (the full table is the default). Officers set the usual one in the settings menu.
  • The start form's Roster button adds a live roster under the post.
  • Members pick their role from the menu in the thread (or type its number). You can type 14 @member to sign someone up and -14 @member to free them.
  • /cta claimfor — take a role over, or free one, when the shortcut above won't do.
  • People on the fill list say which kinds of role they'd take. When most roles are gone, press Place fills at the end of the thread: I suggest who takes which open role, you change any row, and the Place button gives them all their roles at once (they're pinged).
  • Wrong title or time? /signup-edit inside the thread: title:, start_time:20:00 (or none), note:Meet at the bank or board: (roster look). The post, thread, reminder and server event follow; nobody is pinged.
  • The 📋 line on the signup post counts the fill and bomb lists live. The reminders (by default 1 hour, 30 and 15 minutes before, and at the start) ping the same role as the post, in the channel, with a link to the thread; officers can change the times, who is pinged and where. Bomb squads are counted, not pinged.
  • /offtimer start — the same for side content.
  • /disarray — how many players a Disarray level takes. Type players: for your group's level, add enemy: to see who is held back and by how much, and mounts: (like 4 chariot, 2 rhino) for battle mounts, which add levels above 15 points. With nothing typed it shows every level.

After it ends

  • /cta log — enter the loot and repair cost (officers). Add preview:True first to see the split without saving anything; Add from battles on that preview puts everyone from the battleboard fights into it, and Read screenshots reads the bank tab's Est. Market Value, the castle silver bags and the party list from pictures posted in the channel (nothing is used until you pick it).
  • /cta import — pull in who came, from the sheet or battleboard fights. Leave battles empty and tick every fight of the timer from a list (a timer is often more than one fight; each fight shows how many of your guild's players were in it, so someone's other content is easy to skip), or paste links for fights the list doesn't show yet. Also add the signups (or source:Both) offers everyone who signed up or fought, so nobody is missed; you can skip a group or leave people out. You see a preview first. With alt shares on, a member gets one loot share for each of their registered characters in the fights (shown as ×2 in the preview).
  • Albion is slow to list a fight? Press Remind me later in the picker (30 minutes up to 24 hours, or a time you type) and the bot pings you and checks again. Or /cta battlewatch: give it a UTC time range and a minimum player count (and how many of them should be yours), and it looks every 30 minutes and pings you when fights in that range turn up. It only pings; you still review and confirm who is added.
  • /lootcheck — give it the Loot Logger's export and the guild chest log to see loot that never reached the chest, listed guild first, then alliance, then everyone else. It also checks the killboard, so loot someone died carrying shows as lost on death, not missing. Press Open a loot thread to get a thread with a card per player: Retry deathlog search (the killboard can be late) and Resolved. Officers and the logistics role can use it; only they see the answer.
  • /cta close event_id:12 — run it inside the thread: it closes sign-ups, finds who didn't show and posts the recap.
  • /regear claim or /regear kit — pay back anyone who died.
  • /pay — pay in game, then record it: player: for all they're due, event_id: for a CTA, or Mark shares paid on its card.

🔒 Officers: setting up and keeping it running

For officers (the officer role, or anyone with Manage Server).

First time

  • /help with the First-time setup topic — a checklist that ticks itself from your real settings and changes them with buttons. Its 🚀 Quick start button fills in the basics for a new guild once you confirm a preview.
  • Much of the bot only works for registered members. Get everyone to /register; you can register anyone yourself with /registration other.
  • /welcome — see the greeting new people get. Turn it on and write your own wording in the settings menu, which can also post a note after each bot update.
  • /settings panel — post the settings menu (see "Tune it"), then open Officers & roles to choose who counts as an officer, who the callers are, and the logistics role that handles loot logs.
  • Settings menu › Logs & reports › Loot thread reminders — pings the logistics role every few days while a loot thread still has loot unresolved.
  • Settings menu › CTAs & offtimers for your Google Sheet and its composition tabs (the CTA sheet tabs and Offtimer sheet tabs buttons) and the builds tab: /builds post puts a comp's builds in front of members, /builds send DMs one.
  • /registration settings — opens the Registration page of the settings menu: the roles /register hands out, nicknames, alerts, the daily reminder and the screenshot backup. Audits have their own page. Servers that only need registration are added with its Register-only servers button. Loot & payouts › One loot share per character pays for members' alts.

Tune it

  • /settings panel — post the settings menu in an officers-only channel: one permanent message with a button for each part of the bot. This is the one place settings are changed. Click a button, pick a setting, click its choice; every change saves at once. It covers roles, loot, regear, CTAs, registration, reports, battles, the welcome message and prices.
  • /regear ceiling set — the most each role can be regeared.
  • /regear panel — let members file their own regear with a button: choose the channel, private or public threads and the regear officer roles (they approve and mark paid like officers). Each request is a thread with the death, attendance, history and wallet; Approve at guild rate files it in one click, Approve & mark paid records a payment you already sent (the bot never sends silver).
  • /officer — the jobs you do most as buttons: Start a CTA, Log a CTA, Member tools (pay, add silver, register, time off), Pay all approved, Battle report, inbox, settings and Member home (the members' buttons). Post it with channel:, or right-click a member > Apps > Officer tools.
  • /inbox — one private page with everything waiting on an officer: approved regears and loot shares to pay, requests to review, loot threads with players still owing, the Siphoned Energy upload. Mark approved regears as paid… records a batch you have already sent.
  • /digest — preview the weekly officer summary. Switch it on, and the optional DMs to members owed silver, in the settings menu.
  • /siphon upload — every couple of weeks, copy the guild's Siphoned Energy log out of the game into a .txt file and upload it. You see who has taken more than they put in and can ping them after a preview. The reminder channel is in the settings menu.
  • /reactionroles create — post a panel where members click an emoji to give themselves a role, for content pings. Powerful roles, and the officer and starter roles, are refused.

Keep it healthy

  • /registration audit — re-check everyone's guild or alliance status.
  • /roster inactivity — find members who've gone quiet.
  • /server-status — the bot's health and its last nightly backup.
  • /help with the Help board topic — post this guide in a channel; it updates itself after every bot update.
  • /help with the Guide web page topic — the whole guide as one page for people outside the server: a link that updates itself, if one is set up, and a file to forward.
