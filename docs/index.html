What this bot does

A Discord bot for Albion Online guilds: Calls to Arms, loot splits, regears, registration and payouts, built around your own Google Sheet.

The pages after this one: how a member uses it, how a night runs, officer setup, then every command. Commands only work inside a server where I'm set up — to use me in yours, message Birdman on Discord (thebirdman_94).

What it does

Loot splits — Log a Call to Arms and the bot takes the guild's tax and splits the rest between everyone who came. Nobody is paid until an officer says so. Afterwards a loot check shows loot that never reached the chest.
Regear — Prices a lost item, or a whole death from the killboard, at the cheapest market price for its quality in any city but Caerleon (or its tooltip EST if lower), and caps it by role. Members can file their own with a button; the bot finds the death and keeps checking until it appears.
CTA signup threads — A thread where members claim a numbered role by typing its number: start times, reminders, a standby list, fill and bomb lists, alts, no-show tracking and a recap.
Registration — Members verify their Albion character and get their guild or alliance role and nickname. Much of the bot relies on it, and officers can register anyone themselves. Audits find people who left.
Wallets and payouts — Every player has a wallet; officers pay out and everything is on record.
Personal pages — /me shows a member their attendance, streak, sign-ups, wallet and Siphoned Energy balance. /alt links their other characters.
Extras — A welcome message for newcomers, click-an-emoji role panels, a weekly officer summary, Siphoned Energy tracking from the game's log, offtimers (side content), weekly fame tracking, battleboard summaries, giveaways, recruiter rewards, an Excel report and nightly backups.

Three ways to use it in a server

Full bot — Everything above, with that server's own separate data, settings and officers.
Register-only — Just registration: members verify their character and officers look after the roles.
CTA signups only — Just the numbered signup threads, built from the guild's own Google Sheet.

👤 For members

The few things most members ever do.

Get set up

  • /register — prove which Albion character is yours. You get your guild role and nickname.
  • /alt — link a second character. It can sign up too, and still counts as you. If your guild pays alt shares, each of your characters that was in the battle earns you its own loot share.
  • Registering matters: it's how the bot knows which Albion character is yours, so you're found when attendance is imported and your alts and page work. Stuck? An officer can register you. Until you do, the bot reminds you now and then (at most once a day) when you sign up or use a command.

Sign up for a CTA

  • In the signup thread, type a role's number to claim it, e.g. 5. Type -5 to give it back.
  • Every role taken? Type s for standby: you get pinged when one opens, and you claim it by typing its number (-s leaves).
  • Taking whatever's left? Type fill, then make sure to claim a role for attendance and loot split; you're pinged at the start if one is still open. In a separate bomb squad that shares the loot split? Type bomb. -fill and -bomb leave.
  • Signing up on an alt? Type 5 AltName.

Check your own numbers

  • /me — your attendance, streak, sign-ups, wallet and, if your guild tracks it, your Siphoned Energy balance (what you put in minus what you took out). Only you can see it. /me owed_dms:Off stops the weekly “you're owed silver” message.
  • /balance show — your wallet. /balance owed — what you're owed.
  • /cta info — how a CTA's loot was split. /balance leaderboard — the top players.
  • /price — what an item costs in every city.

If you died

  • Press File a regear on your guild's regear panel (or File a regear for this battle under a battle post). Pick your character, and the death if you have several. A thread opens for you and the officers with the killboard details. If the death isn't on the killboard yet it is checked again after 24 hours and then daily for a week, and you're pinged when it turns up. After a week, post a screenshot in the thread. You need to be registered first.

Choose what you get pinged for

  • Your guild may have a roles panel: a message with numbered emojis next to roles. Click an emoji to get that role (and its pings), click it again to drop it.

🎙️ Running a CTA

For officers, and anyone with the starter role (the callers). A typical night, in order.

Before it starts

  • /cta start — pick the composition tab. Add start_time:19:00 (UTC) for a reminder and a server event.
  • Add format:Roles only to show just the number and the role, or leave it for the full table with weapons and gear. Your officers choose the usual one in the settings menu.
  • Members type their role's number in the thread. You can type 14 @member to sign someone up and -14 @member to free them.
  • /cta claimfor — take a role over, or free one, when the shortcut above won't do.
  • Wrong title or time? /signup-edit inside the thread: title:, start_time:20:00 (or none to remove it) or note:Meet at the bank. The post, thread name, reminder and server event all follow, and nobody is pinged.
  • The 📋 line on the signup post counts the fill and bomb lists live. The reminders (by default 1 hour, 30 and 15 minutes before, and at the start) ping the same role as the post, in the channel, with a link to the thread; officers can change the times, who is pinged and where. Bomb squads are counted, not pinged.
  • /offtimer start — the same for side content.

After it ends

  • /cta log — enter the loot and repair cost (officers). Add preview:True first to see the split without saving anything; Add from battles on that preview puts everyone from the battleboard fights into it, and Read screenshots reads the bank tab's Est. Market Value, the castle silver bags and the party list from pictures posted in the channel (nothing is used until you pick it).
  • /cta importmassup — pull in who came, from the sheet or battleboard fights. Leave battles empty and tick every fight of the timer from a list (a timer is often more than one fight; each fight shows how many of your guild's players were in it, so someone's other content is easy to skip), or paste links for fights the list doesn't show yet. Also add the signups (or source:Both) offers everyone who signed up or fought, so nobody is missed; you can skip a group or leave people out. You see a preview first. With alt shares on, a member gets one loot share for each of their registered characters in the fights (shown as ×2 in the preview).
  • Albion is slow to list a fight? Press Remind me later in the picker (30 minutes up to 24 hours, or a time you type) and the bot pings you and checks again. Or /cta battlewatch: give it a UTC time range and a minimum player count (and how many of them should be yours), and it looks every 30 minutes and pings you when fights in that range turn up. It only pings; you still review and confirm who is added.
  • /lootcheck — give it the Loot Logger's export and the guild chest log to see loot that never reached the chest, listed guild first, then alliance, then everyone else. It also checks the killboard, so loot someone died carrying shows as lost on death, not missing. Press Open a loot thread to get a thread with a card per player: Retry deathlog search (the killboard can be late) and Resolved. Officers and the logistics role can use it; only they see the answer.
  • /cta close event_id:12 — run it inside the thread: it closes sign-ups, finds who didn't show and posts the recap.
  • /regear claim or /regear kit — pay back anyone who died.
  • /pay — pay people in game, then record it here: player: for everything they're owed, event_id: for a whole CTA.

🔒 Officers: setting up and keeping it running

For officers (the officer role, or anyone with Manage Server).

First time

  • /help with the First-time setup topic — a checklist that ticks itself from your real settings. Its 🚀 Quick start button fills in the basics for a new guild after showing every change and asking you to confirm.
  • Much of the bot only works for registered members. Get everyone to /register; you can register anyone yourself with /register-other.
  • /welcome — see the greeting new people get. Turn it on and write your own wording in the settings menu, which can also post a note after each bot update.
  • /settings panel — post the settings menu (see "Tune it"), then open Officers & roles to choose who counts as an officer, who the callers are, and the logistics role that handles loot logs.
  • Settings menu › Regear & logs › Loot thread reminders — pings the logistics role every few days while a loot thread still has loot unresolved.
  • Settings menu › CTAs & offtimers for your Google Sheet and its composition tabs (the CTA sheet tabs and Offtimer sheet tabs buttons).
  • /registration-settings — opens the Registration page of the settings menu: the roles /register hands out, nicknames, alerts, the nightly audit and the daily reminder. Other servers that only need registration are added with its Register-only servers button. Loot & payouts › One loot share per character pays for members' alts.

Tune it

  • /settings panel — post the settings menu in an officers-only channel: one permanent message with a button for each part of the bot. This is the one place settings are changed. Click a button, pick a setting, click its choice; every change saves at once. It covers roles, loot splits, the regear rate, CTAs and recaps, regear requests, registration, reports, battles, the welcome message and market prices.
  • /regear ceiling set — the most each role can be regeared.
  • /regear panel — let members file their own regear with a button: choose the channel, private or public threads and the regear officer roles (they approve and mark paid like officers). Each request is a thread with the death, attendance, history and wallet; Approve at guild rate files it in one click, Approve & mark paid records a payment you already sent (the bot never sends silver). The menu can also let the bot handle clear-cut ones itself.
  • /officer — the jobs you do most as buttons: Log a CTA or an offtimer (a short form, then pick who was there), Member tools (pay what's owed, add silver, register, time off, fame tracking), Battle watch, inbox, settings. Post it with channel:, or right-click a member > Apps > Officer tools.
  • /inbox — one private page with everything waiting on an officer: approved regears and loot shares to pay, requests to review, loot threads with players still owing, the Siphoned Energy upload. Mark approved regears as paid… records a batch you have already sent, after listing the claims.
  • /digest — preview the weekly officer summary. Switch it on, and the optional DMs to members owed silver, in the settings menu.
  • /siphon upload — every couple of weeks, copy the guild's Siphoned Energy log out of the game into a .txt file and upload it. You see who has taken more than they put in and can ping them after a preview. The reminder channel is in the settings menu.
  • /reactionroles create — post a panel where members click an emoji to give themselves a role, for content pings. Powerful roles, and the officer and starter roles, are refused.

Keep it healthy

  • /register-audit — re-check everyone's guild or alliance status.
  • /roster inactivity — find members who've gone quiet.
  • /server-status — the bot's health and its last nightly backup.
  • /help with the Help board topic — post this guide in a channel; it updates itself after every bot update.
  • /help with the Guide web page topic — the whole guide as one page for people outside the server: a link that updates itself, if one is set up, and a file to forward.

Registration

Members prove which Albion character is theirs and get their Discord roles.

🟢 /register [character_name]
Verify your Albion character and get your guild / alliance role. Everyone starts here.

🔒 /register-other <player> <character_name>
Register someone else on their behalf, so a member who is stuck never blocks you.

🟡 /alt <action> [character_name] [player]
Link your other Albion characters, so they can sign up for CTAs and still count as you (and earn their own loot share, if the guild pays alt shares). Officers can manage anyone's.

🔒 /register-check <player> [live_check]
Everything on record for one person: character, alts, affiliation, when they registered.

🔒 /register-override <player> <character_name> <membership_type> [notes] [exempt_days]
Register someone by hand when the Albion API gets it wrong.

🔒 /register-guide [image] [text] [clear] [guild_id]
Set the picture and wording shown when someone types !register.

🔒 /welcome
See the greeting new people get when they join the Discord (where to apply, how to register) next to its settings, which live in the settings menu.

🔒 /registration-settings
Open the registration settings here: guild and ally roles, nicknames, alerts, the nightly audit and reminders. On the bot's own server it also sets up the register-only servers.

🟡 /settings striproles <action> [role]
Extra roles /register-audit takes away from people who are no longer eligible. Anyone can list; officers add or remove.

CTA signups

Numbered signup threads for a Call to Arms, built from a composition tab in your sheet.

🎟️ /cta start <tab> [title] [ping_role] [channel] [+2 more]
Post a CTA ping and open a numbered signup thread. Add a start time (UTC) for reminders and a server event. format: shows roles only, or roles and builds.

🎟️ /cta close [event_id]
Stop new claims in a CTA thread (run it inside the thread). With event_id: also lists no-shows and posts a recap.

🎟️ /cta claimfor <action> <claim_number> [member]
Claim a role for someone else, or release one (run it inside the thread).

🎟️ /signup-edit [title] [start_time] [note]

🔒 /cta starter-role [role] [clear]
Choose which role may run /cta start on this server.

CTA loot & attendance

Log an event's loot, record who came, and split it. Only on the bot's own server and full-bot servers.

🔒 /cta log [name] [loot] [repair_cost] [+8 more]
Log a Call to Arms: loot, repair and attendees. No options gives a form (loot can be left blank). On the preview, Add from battles and Read screenshots fill things in. Nothing is saved until Log it.

🔒 /cta attendance <action> <event_id> <players>
Add players to an event's attendance, or remove them.

🎟️ /lootcheck <loot_log> <chest_log> [player] [min_value] [ignore_below] [+3 more]
Who picked up loot that never reached the chest (guild, then alliance, then outsiders). Loot someone died carrying counts as lost on death. Opens a loot thread. Officers and logistics role.

🔒 /cta importmassup <event_id> [comp_sheet_gid] [source] [battles]
Add attendees from the signup sheet and/or battleboard fights. Both adds everyone on either list, so nobody is missed. Tick the fights from a list or be reminded later; preview, then confirm.

🔒 /cta battlewatch
Watch a UTC time range with a minimum player count (and how many of yours) and get pinged when fights in it show up. See and cancel your reminders and watches.

🔒 /cta edit <event_id> [loot] [repair_cost] [tax_rate] [+7 more]
Fix anything on a logged event: loot, silver bags, repair, percentages, name, date, people, fights, late loot. On its own it opens an Edit menu. Every change is remembered.

🟢 /cta info [event_id] [limit]
One event's full loot split, who signed up but didn't show, and what was changed after it was logged. With no event_id: the recent events.

🔒 /cta delete <event_id>
Permanently delete an event and its attendance (asks first).

Siphoned Energy

Who put in more Siphoned Energy than they took out, from the game's log. Only on the bot's own server and full-bot servers.

🎟️ /siphon upload <file>
Upload the Siphoned Energy log copied from the game (a .txt). Only new lines are counted, so overlapping uploads are safe. Shows who is behind, with buttons to ping them or undo.

🎟️ /siphon report [player] [days] [post]
Everyone's balance (put in minus taken out): who is behind, who is ahead, with a spreadsheet file. Or open one player's page.

🎟️ /siphon ping [behind_by] [channel]
Name the members who are behind in a channel. You see who first and confirm before anything is sent.

🎟️ /siphon history [undo]
The uploads so far. Undo the newest one.

🔒 /siphon exempt [member] [name] [remove]
Leave someone out of the pings (the guild's crafter, say), or put them back.

🔒 /siphon adjust <amount> [member] [name] [reason]
Add to or take from someone's balance by hand, e.g. energy paid in before the first log.

Regear

Price a death from live market data (or the killboard), cap it by role, and file the claim.

🔒 /regear claim <player> <role> <item_id> [item_name] [category] [event_id] [+3 more]
File a regear claim at the cheapest market price for the item's quality (any city but Caerleon, equal-power tiers), capped by role. Optional EST.

🔒 /regear kit [player] [character] [role] [+2 more]
Price a player's whole death from the killboard, each item at its own quality, and file their regear. Enter an EST or pick another quality if needed.

🔒 /regear panel [channel] [visibility] [ping] [post]
Set up member regear requests: the File a regear button, private or public threads, who reviews them. Members file, the bot finds the death and rechecks daily; you approve in one click.

🔒 /regear claimcustom <player> <amount> <reason> [event_id] [notes]
File a claim for an amount you type -- no lookup, no cap.

🟢 /regear info [claim_id] [status] [limit]
One claim in full. With no claim_id: a list of claims.

🔒 /regear void <claim_id>
Delete an unpaid claim so it can be refiled correctly.

🔒 /regear ceiling set <role> <amount> [category]
Set the most silver a role can be regeared (optionally per item category).

🔒 /regear ceiling remove <role> [category]
Remove a role's ceiling.

🔒 /regear ceiling copy <from_role> <to_role>
Copy every ceiling from one role to another.

🟢 /regear ceiling list [role]
Show the role ceilings.

🟢 /price <item> [quality]
What an item costs right now in every city, from the Albion Online Data Project.

Payouts & balances

Pay people, and track the wallet balance, the guild bank and the reports.

🔒 /inbox
Everything waiting on an officer on one private page: payouts to send (with a button to mark approved regears paid), regear requests to review, loot threads, the Siphoned Energy upload.

🔒 /pay [player] [event_id] [claim_id] [+2 more]
Record a payout: all a player is owed (player), a whole event (event_id), one claim (claim_id) or part of a wallet (player + amount). The member menu's Pay what's owed does the first.

🟢 /balance show [player] [everyone] [history] [limit]
What a player is owed and has been paid, by source (loot, regear, wallet) and where the wallet came from. Also their history, or everyone with a balance.

🟢 /balance owed [player]
Everything owed to a player: pending loot and regear plus wallet balance.

🟢 /balance fund [project_events]
The guild bank / regear fund's health, or a projection at other tax rates.

🟡 /balance leaderboard [limit] [by] [days]
Top players by silver received or CTA attendance. Officers can also rank no-shows.

🔒 /digest
Preview this week's officer summary (CTAs, offtimers, what's owed, new members) and see when it posts. The summary and the optional owed-silver DMs are set up in the settings menu.

🔒 /balance add <amount> [player] [role] [notes]
Credit silver to one player's wallet, or to everyone holding a role.

🔒 /balance closeout
Guild closing: clear unpaid regears and zero every wallet.

🔒 /export
Generate a fresh loot / regear report (.xlsx).

Offtimers

🔒 /offtimer delete <event_id>

🟢 /offtimer info [event_id] [limit]
One offtimer event's breakdown. With no event_id: the recent ones.
